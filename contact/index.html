Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about my music, process, and licensing policies

🎵 Can I license your tracks for film/games?

Yes. I am fully independent and own all sync/master rights to my discography. Please drop me an email stating your project details, and I can arrange licensing options.

🤝 How do we coordinate a collaboration?

Shoot me an email detailing your background, links to your current work, and what you have in mind. I love combining electronic synths with interesting organic elements and vocal drafts.

🎥 Do you accept video sponsorship or collaboration videos?

I am open to reviewing gear showcases, synth collaborations, and joint tutorials for YouTube (Studio J) and Bilibili, provided they align with high-quality synthesizer craft.

🔄 Can I download stems for remixing?

I occasionally release stems on my Bandcamp page for community remix events. For specific song requests, send a message explaining your plans.
