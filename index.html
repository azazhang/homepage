🎨 Process

Behind the Knobs

My process is about physical connection with synths and music gear. I compose, arrange, and mix every track using hardware controllers and Ableton Live.

Every track under the JJJ_B name is composed and mixed from my desktop workspace. I explore ambient textures that combine live looping and FM synthesis with organic keyboard performances, keeping the raw human touch in electronic soundscapes.

Through studio videos on YouTube (Studio J) and Bilibili, I share my workspace, unboxings, and live looping workflows. It's about opening the studio door and sharing how the music is actually structured, track by track.
