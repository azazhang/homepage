Super Velocity Curve

Per-Pad MIDI Velocity Curves for Finger Drummers & Controllers

When your DAW's single global velocity curve isn't enough. Super Velocity Curve is a dedicated audio plugin and standalone app that lets you set independent velocity dynamics, response curves, floor/ceiling thresholds, and note remapping for every individual pad or MIDI key.

🎯
Per-Pad Curves Linear, exponential, log & floor/ceiling gates per note
🎛️
Controller Profiles Launchpad, Maschine, Yamaha FGDP, SPD-SX, GM
🧙
Calibration Wizard Hit histograms & interactive playing dynamic analysis
🔌
Universal Formats VST3, AU, CLAP & Standalone (macOS & Windows)
